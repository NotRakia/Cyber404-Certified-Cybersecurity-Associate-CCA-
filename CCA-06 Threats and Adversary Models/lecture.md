## Social Engineering
- Phishing, pretexting and independent verification; do not require browsing suspicious infrastructure.
## Common Abuse
- Malware concepts, credential stuffing, password spraying, persistence and misuse of legitimate tools.
## Models
- Kill-chain stages, Diamond Model features and Pyramid of Pain as thinking aids with limitations. They are not standards or automatic attribution engines.
## ATT&CK
- Tactic as objective and technique as method. Use version-specific names; freeze v20.0 as this release's taxonomy baseline rather than carrying the old v19 assumption forward.\
## Hypotheses
- Compare credential abuse with stale service credentials and other benign explanations; state the
next evidence required.
