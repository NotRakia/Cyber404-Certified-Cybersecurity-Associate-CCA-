## Windows Identity and Events
 - Local/domain accounts, successful and failed logons, Security provider event IDs 4624/4625 and logon types 2, 3, 5, and 10.
## Endpoint Telemetry
 - Sysmon process creation, network observations and collection configuration. Do not join unrelated provider IDs or PIDs.
## Linux Basics
- Filesystem paths, users/groups, owner/group/others, rwx permissions, processes and safe read-only inspection.
## Elementary Scripting
- Parse JSON/CSV, filter conditions, count records, sort by normalized time and preserve an unchanged source.
## Evidence Limits
- A normalized teaching record is not a raw EVTX export; a process name does not establish human identity or malicious intent.
