## Readiness
- Roles, authority, escalation, evidence handling and business priorities.
## Investigation and response
- Triage, containment, eradication and recovery actions with purpose and tradeoffs. Teach NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 within CSF 2.0; older phase diagrams can be historical aids, not its current exact model.
## Continuity
- RTO versus RPO, backup versus restore assurance and critical dependencies.
## Communication
- Concise facts, impact, limitations, actions, next decisions and documented closure criteria; perform a lessons-learned review.
