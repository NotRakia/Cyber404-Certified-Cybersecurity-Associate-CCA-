## 1. While reviewing a classroom exercise, an analyst suspects account misuse. What should guide the next response step?
a. The longest possible command\
b. An unsupported attribution guess\
c. Documented authority, impact and an evidence-based response procedure\
d. A desire to delete all logs immediately\
Answer: c.

## 2. While reviewing a classroom exercise, what is the main aim of containment during an incident?
a. Publicly name an attacker immediately\
b. Limit ongoing harm and spread while supporting investigation and continuity\
c. Guarantee every root cause is already removed\
d. Replace restoration procedures\
Answer: b.

## 3. While reviewing a classroom exercise, which action most closely supports eradication?
a. Remove the confirmed persistence mechanism and address its cause\
b. Only publish an incident summary\
c. Only measure backup size\
d. Ignore the compromised credential\
Answer: a.
