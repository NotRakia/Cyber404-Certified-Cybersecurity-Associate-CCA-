## Pipeline
- Event generation, transport, parsing, normalization, storage, querying, alerting and ingestion delay.
## Triage
- Event/alert/incident distinctions, evidence scoping, cross-source correlation, baselines and uncertainty.
## Detection quality
TP/FP/FN/TN, precision and recall; require explicit ground truth, denominator and zero- denominator handling.
## Intelligence
- Indicator timeliness, confidence and context; an IP match is a lead, not a human identity.
## Tuning
- Narrow validated exceptions and monitor coverage rather than disabling entire detection categories.
