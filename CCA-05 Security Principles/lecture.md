## CIA and Risk Vocabulary
- Confidentiality, integrity, availability, asset, threat, weakness and impact.
## AAA
- Authentication, authorization and accounting as distinct functions. Practice an authenticated-but-denied access case.
## Controls
- Administrative, physical and technical examples; preventive, detective and corrective functions can coexist.
## Access and Architecture
- Least privilege, defense in depth, secure defaults and Zero Trust resource-access decisions.
## Reasoning
- Apply a model to an observed fact without assigning more certainty than the record supports.
