## Web Flow
- URL, hostname, request method, path, response status, headers, and message content. Compare GET and HEAD in actual captured records.
## DNS
- A, AAAA, CNAME, MX and TXT roles; query/answer versus later use. TTL and caching are introduced without assuming they prove compromise.
## Email
- Envelope versus display identity; header context; SPF/DKIM/DMARC purpose and limits. Passing domain authentication is not proof a request is honest.
## Data Handling
- Structured records, JSON types, encodings and safe parsing; do not execute log content.
