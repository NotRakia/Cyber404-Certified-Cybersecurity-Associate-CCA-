## Lifecyle
- Joiner/mover/leaver, dedicated service identities, access reviews and administrative separation.
## Authentication
- Distinct factor categories, MFA and phishing-resistant cryptographic authenticators; two secrets are not automatically two factors.
## Authorization
- RBAC, ABAC, deny by default and objective-level permission checks on every request.
## Sessions
- Passwords, sessions and tokens have distinct lifecycle. Disabling an account does not itself prove all active access artifacts were revoked.
